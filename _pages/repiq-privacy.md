*Last updated: September 21, 2026*

RepIQ is a workout-logging app published by Virtual Platform Consulting ("vpcguru", "we", "us"). This policy explains what data RepIQ collects and how it's used.

## What we collect

- **Account identity:** if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an email address (or Apple's private relay address) and a unique account ID, used solely to identify your account.
- **Workout data:** the exercises, sets, reps, and weights you log, so your history and personal records sync across your devices.
- **Photos (optional):** if you use the camera/photo-import feature to digitize a paper workout program, that image is processed to extract text and is not stored after import.

You can also use RepIQ anonymously without signing in. In that mode, workout data is tied to a device-local account with no email or identity attached.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your data for advertising or tracking across apps or websites.
- We do not share your data with third parties except the infrastructure providers listed below, who process it only to run the app.

## Where data is stored

Account authentication and workout data are stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase's own privacy policy governs their handling of this data as our processor: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated workout data at any time from within the app (Account > Delete account). This immediately and permanently removes your data from our systems — it cannot be undone.

## Children's privacy

RepIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to [vpcguru@gmail.com](mailto:vpcguru@gmail.com).
